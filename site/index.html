How it works

Three steps, no tunnels.

  1. 01

    Arm the fast lane

    One tap starts the on-device DNS engine and boost locks. Android asks for VPN permission once — that's the engine's TUN interface, not a VPN tunnel to anywhere.

  2. 02

    Launch your game

    Kaya detects the matchmaker lookup (activision, tencent, garena…) and races resolvers head-to-head. The winner gets remembered for next time.

  3. 03

    Play on the fastest edge

    The game connects to the IP the fastest resolver returned — usually the local region you actually wanted. Watch the HUD drop.

Honesty box: Kaya can't beat physics or fix a bad ISP. Where the resolver chain detours through Europe (common on some African carriers), gains are real. Where your network already routes cleanly, they're not. Everything else a "booster" promises is a VPN with extra steps.

Honest FAQ

Straight answers.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch, no ads, no accounts. It's open source — read every line before you install it. There is no business model, and that's the point.

Will this get me banned?

Kaya changes how your phone resolves names and holds radio locks. It doesn't inject, modify or read game traffic — the same class of thing a custom DNS setting does. Always check your game's current rules; some publishers are stricter than others.

Why does Play Protect warn me?

Because Kaya isn't on the Play Store — Google shows that card for every sideloaded app, famous or not. The APK is signed with Kaya's own release key and every release ships SHA-256 checksums you can verify.

How much ping will I actually save?

Depends on your ISP. Where the resolver chain detours through Europe, gains are real and noticeable. Where your network already routes cleanly, expect little. The app shows measured numbers, not marketing ones — and the Network screen's game benchmark gives you before/after numbers on your own phone.

Is there an iPhone (IPA) version?

Not yet, honestly. iOS has no VpnService equivalent that a cross-platform app can use — Kaya's engine needs a native Swift Packet Extension rewrite, and Apple requires macOS + paid developer signing to build an installable IPA. There is no IPA today; any site offering one is not Kaya.

Will Game Focus block my messages?

No. It uses Android's Do Not Disturb in priority mode: phone calls and allowlisted apps (WhatsApp by default) still come through; everything else is silenced for the match and automatically restored after. Kaya never reads or stores message content.
